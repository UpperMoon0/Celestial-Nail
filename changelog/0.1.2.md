# 0.1.2

- Fix shader replacement compatibility by using vanilla entity and emissive render passes with real atlas UVs.
- Apply vanilla lightmap and transformed-normal lighting to the shell; rotate fragment normals during removal.
- Render pulse strips and the impact shockwave as ordinary geometry. Vanilla/shader-pack fog now applies; the former custom refraction/star shader is retired from production.
- Require Perfomant Boom 1.1.3 or newer within the 1.x series on all five loader targets, and build against 1.1.3.

These notes describe source changes, not a published release.
