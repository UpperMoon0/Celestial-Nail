# Celestial Nail 0.1.0

- Add persistent administrator-controlled Nails with summon, launch, list and remove commands.
- Add animated cyan rifts, crystal-and-stone models, sound, shockwaves and a lasting embedded monument.
- Support Fabric/Forge 1.20.1, Fabric/NeoForge 1.21.1 and NeoForge 26.1.2.
- Require matching Perfomant Boom 1.1.0 for bounded terrain clearing, fluid cleanup and boundary repair.
- Preserve saved impact progress and chunk ownership; removing a Nail cancels future damage and clearing.
- Impacts intentionally bypass normal explosion/player-break protection and suppress terrain/container drops. Back up worlds before use.
