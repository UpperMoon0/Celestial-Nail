# 0.1.3

- Add opt-in, rate-limited render diagnostics with actual shader program, color-write masks, framebuffers and RGB draw probes.
- Keep tall Nails visible when their buried anchor's terrain section is culled; preserve normal frustum, distance and depth checks.
- Keep the entire Nail self-lit with vanilla emissive entity passes, so a buried or unlit anchor cannot darken its body.
- Start the normal crumble lifecycle when descent enters the void, without creating an impact at the world bottom; preserve intentionally deep embedded Nails.
- Add 40 real Forge 1.20.1 rendering cases with Oculus 1.8.0 and Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3, including close/inside/underside hover views, dark pinned-state brightness, terrain occlusion and deliberate rendering failures.
- Provide a CI workflow patch for the live suite and retain local image/log evidence. Keep the 0.1.2 vanilla entity/emissive renderer and Boom 1.1.3+ dependency.

These notes describe source changes, not a published release.
